Lucerna

Lucerna brings the Bible to your Lock Screen and Home Screen with simple, beautiful Scripture Widgets.

The word “Lucerna” comes from Latin, meaning “lamp” or “light.” The name reflects the purpose of the app: to keep Scripture close, visible, and quietly present throughout your day. Not hidden behind menus. Not buried in notifications. Just a small light on your screen, pointing you back to God’s Word.

Lucerna was created with a simple ethos: God’s Word should not be locked behind a subscription. This app will never ask you for money. No subscriptions. No premium tiers. No paid unlocks. No artificial limits.

Whether you want a daily verse, a passage to memorize, or a quiet reminder of truth on your screen, Lucerna gives you a simple way to carry Scripture with you.
