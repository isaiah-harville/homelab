A Small Light

The word “Lucerna” comes from Latin, meaning “lamp” or “light.” The app keeps Scripture close, visible, and quietly present throughout your day.

Never a Subscription

God’s Word should not be locked behind a subscription. Lucerna has no premium tiers, paid unlocks, or artificial limits.
