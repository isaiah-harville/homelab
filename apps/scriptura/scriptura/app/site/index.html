Scriptura

The Bible on your Lock Screen and Home Screen — simple, beautiful Scripture Widgets.

“Scriptura” is Latin for “writing,” the root of “Scripture” itself. The app keeps God’s written Word close, visible, and quietly present throughout your day — not hidden behind menus, not buried in notifications. Just the text itself, on your screen, pointing you back to it.

Curate albums of verses that rotate automatically, read any book offline in a full Bible reader, memorize Scripture with guided practice, and study the Greek, Hebrew, and Latin text word by word.
