Scriptura

Scriptura brings the Bible to your Lock Screen and Home Screen with simple, beautiful Scripture Widgets.

The word “Scriptura” comes from Latin, meaning “writing” — the root of “Scripture” itself. The name reflects the purpose of the app: to keep God’s written Word close, visible, and quietly present throughout your day. Not hidden behind menus. Not buried in notifications. Just the text itself, on your screen, pointing you back to it.

Scriptura was created with a simple ethos: God’s Word should not be locked behind a subscription. This app will never ask you for money. No subscriptions. No premium tiers. No paid unlocks. No artificial limits.

Whether you want a daily verse, a passage to memorize, or a quiet reminder of truth on your screen, Scriptura gives you a simple way to carry Scripture with you.
